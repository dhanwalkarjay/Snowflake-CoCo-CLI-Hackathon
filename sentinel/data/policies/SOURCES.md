# Policy Document Sources

All regulatory documents used in this project are publicly available from their respective issuing bodies.

| File | Document | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `RBI_Master_Direction.pdf` | Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (Updated) | Reserve Bank of India |
| `RBI guidance on asset classification and SMA categories.pdf` | Master Circular - Prudential norms on Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning | Reserve Bank of India |
| `FATF Recommendations.pdf` | International Standards on Combating Money Laundering and the Financing of Terrorism & Proliferation — The FATF Recommendations | Financial Action Task Force (FATF) |
| `Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratio standard.pdf` | BIS Papers No 164 — The Liquidity Coverage Ratio a decade on: a stocktake of the literature | Bank for International Settlements (BIS) |

These documents are used solely for building a Cortex Search index to ground the agent's policy citations. No proprietary or confidential documents are included.
